Research Profile

Researcher and data scientist working at the intersection of probabilistic reasoning, multi-objective decision making and generative artificial intelligence. Doctoral and post-doctoral research with IBM Research Labs Dublin and United Technology Research Centre produced exact and approximate algorithms for decision problems with imprecise preferences and conflicting objectives, published at AAAI, IJCAI, UAI, CP and ICTAI. Now leads the generative-AI research programme for enterprise wireless networks at Extreme Networks, with nine granted US patents and a record of translating research into production systems.

Research interests  Multi-objective decision making under uncertainty · preference elicitation and inference · probabilistic graphical models and influence diagrams · grounded and retrieval-augmented generative AI · constraint optimisation · machine learning for networked systems.
